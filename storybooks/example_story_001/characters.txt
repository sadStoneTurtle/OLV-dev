Characters of the Whispering Woods

Finn the Fox
- A curious and brave little fox with bright orange fur
- Has a fluffy tail that swishes when excited
- Loves exploring and making new friends
- Lives in a den near the edge of the Whispering Woods
- Learned to be patient during the adventure
- One of the four Heroes of the Whispering Woods

Bella the Rabbit
- Finn's best friend
- Has long ears that perk up when excited
- Lives in a cozy burrow near the Babbling Brook
- Playful and energetic
- Discovered her courage during the adventure to save the forest
- One of the four Heroes of the Whispering Woods

Oliver the Owl
- A wise young owl who can fly and scout ahead
- Perches on branches of the Great Oak Tree
- Learned that asking for help is okay
- Has keen eyesight and can see far distances
- One of the four Heroes of the Whispering Woods

Daisy the Deer
- A gentle and graceful deer
- Has a keen sense of smell
- Strong and kind-hearted
- Realized how strong she truly was during the adventure
- Uses her antlers to help friends
- One of the four Heroes of the Whispering Woods

The Great Oak Tree
- The oldest and wisest tree in the Whispering Woods
- Stands at the heart of the forest
- Can speak to the animals
- Has deep, warm voice
- Branches rustle when speaking
- Protects and guides the forest animals
- Called the four friends to save the Singing Stream

Other Forest Animals
- Wise old owls (Oliver's relatives)
- Playful rabbits (Bella's family)
- Singing birds that make music in the trees
- Gentle deer that roam the forest
- A friendly turtle who gave the friends a riddle
- A family of squirrels who needed help gathering acorns
